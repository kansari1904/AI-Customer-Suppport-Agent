# FlowDesk Pricing and Plans

FlowDesk offers three plans: Free, Pro, and Business. Plans differ in workspace limits, team size, storage, integrations, support, and security features.

## 1. Free Plan

The Free plan is designed for individuals and very small teams that want to try FlowDesk without a paid subscription.

### Free Plan Features

| Feature | Free |
|---|---:|
| Monthly Price | $0 |
| Team Members | Up to 3 |
| Workspaces | 1 |
| Storage | 1 GB |
| Projects | Up to 5 |
| Integrations | 3 |
| Data Export | CSV |
| Two-Factor Authentication | Yes |
| Priority Support | No |
| Uptime SLA | No |

The Free plan does not include priority customer support or a contractual uptime SLA.

---

## 2. Pro Plan

The Pro plan is intended for growing teams that need additional storage, projects, integrations, and collaboration features.

### Pro Plan Features

| Feature | Pro |
|---|---:|
| Monthly Price | $15 per user |
| Team Members | Up to 20 |
| Workspaces | 5 |
| Storage | 50 GB |
| Projects | Unlimited |
| Integrations | 15 |
| Data Export | CSV and JSON |
| Two-Factor Authentication | Yes |
| Priority Support | No |
| Uptime SLA | No |

Pro customers can export workspace data in both CSV and JSON formats. The Pro plan does not include a contractual uptime SLA.

---

## 3. Business Plan

The Business plan is designed for larger organizations that require advanced security, administration, and support capabilities.

### Business Plan Features

| Feature | Business |

Business customers receive priority support and a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA.

# Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

## Which FlowDesk plan is free?

The **Free plan** costs $0 per month and supports up to 3 team members, 1 workspace, 1 GB of storage, and 5 projects.

## What is the cheapest paid FlowDesk plan?

The **Pro plan** is the cheapest paid plan at $15 per user per month.

## Which plan has unlimited team members?

The **Business plan** provides unlimited team members.

## Which plan includes priority support?

Only the **Business plan** includes priority customer support.

## Which plan has an uptime SLA?

Only the **Business plan** includes a contractual **99.9% monthly uptime SLA**.

## Can I export data on the Free plan?

Yes. Free customers can export workspace data in **CSV format**.

## Can Pro customers export JSON?

Yes. The Pro plan supports both **CSV and JSON** data exports.

## What is the difference between Pro and Business?

The Pro plan supports up to 20 team members, 5 workspaces, 50 GB of storage, and 15 integrations. The Business plan provides unlimited team members, workspaces, and integrations, 500 GB of storage, priority support, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Can I upgrade from Free to Pro?

Yes. Workspace administrators can upgrade from the Free plan to Pro through **Settings → Billing → Change Plan**. The Pro plan becomes active immediately after the upgrade.

## Can I upgrade directly from Free to Business?

Yes. Workspace administrators can upgrade directly from the Free plan to the Business plan through **Settings → Billing → Change Plan**.

## Does upgrading immediately give access to Business features?

Yes. After a successful upgrade, Business features become available immediately.

# Important Plan Rules

1. Only workspace administrators can change a subscription plan.
2. Upgrading a subscription activates the new plan immediately.
3. Canceling a subscription prevents the next renewal but does not immediately delete the workspace.
4. The Free and Pro plans do not include a contractual uptime SLA.
5. Business customers receive a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA.
6. Business is the only plan with priority customer support.
7. Free customers can export data in CSV format.
8. Pro and Business customers can export data in both CSV and JSON formats.
