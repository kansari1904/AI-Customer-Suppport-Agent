02 — Refund Policy
Can I get a refund for my FlowDesk subscription?

FlowDesk provides refunds for eligible subscription charges requested within 14 days of the original purchase or renewal. To request a refund, contact support from the email associated with your FlowDesk workspace.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method and typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.
