18 — Pause vs Cancel Subscription
What is the difference between pausing and canceling my subscription?

Pausing temporarily stops your paid subscription for up to 90 days while keeping your workspace and data available for later use.

Canceling stops future subscription renewals and moves the workspace to the Free plan after the current billing period ends.

If you expect to return soon, pausing is generally more appropriate. If you no longer need paid features, cancellation is the better option.
